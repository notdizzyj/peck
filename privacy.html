English Version

Peck is a screenshot organizer and search app that runs completely on your iPhone without requiring an internet connection. This Privacy Policy matches the in-app Settings › Data & Privacy screen.

1. Data Not Collected

  • Peck has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or personal identifiers.
  • Peck never uploads your screenshots, thumbnails, recognized text, search queries, titles, summaries, favorites, or edits to our servers or any third party.
  • Peck contains zero advertising SDKs, zero analytics services, and zero cross-app tracking (NSPrivacyTracking = false, NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes = []).

2. Photo Library & Manual Imports

  • Last-30-days automatic organizing & on-demand older screenshots: Only after you grant Photos access does Peck read images flagged as screenshots within the scope you allow (Full Access or Limited Selected Photos). To keep things fast and kind to your battery, automatic organizing focuses on screenshots from the last 30 days; older screenshots (> 30 days) appear only as lightweight thumbnail cards and are organized locally on demand only when you tap them.
  • Manual imports & clipboard paste: Images you manually pick via the system photo picker or paste from your clipboard bypass the 30-day automatic window and are kept as separate local copies inside Peck's app container. Revoking Photos access clears automatically scanned photo caches while keeping manually imported copies until you remove or clear them.
  • iCloud Photos: If you use Apple's iCloud Photos, iOS PhotoKit may download originals from your own iCloud library to your device according to your system settings. Peck never sends images to third-party servers.

3. Local Processing & Storage Protection

  • Text reading, summarizing, organizing, and full-text search all run locally on your iPhone—even in Airplane Mode.
  • Local SQLite databases, thumbnails, and imported copies use iOS Complete File Protection and are excluded from device backups (isExcludedFromBackup = true).
  • You can enable App Lock (Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode) in Settings; Peck covers the interface automatically when inactive or in the app switcher.

4. System Permissions

  • Photos: Used to discover screenshots within the scope you grant. You may also decline library access and add individual images manually.
  • Reminders: Requested only when you explicitly tap Save on a reminder you reviewed; Peck never reads or bulk-modifies your reminders.
  • Calendar: Uses Apple's out-of-process system event editor (EKEventEditViewController) so you always confirm before saving; Peck does not request Calendar read permission or auto-write events.
  • Face ID / Biometrics: Used solely to unlock your local library when you enable App Lock. Biometric data stays in the iOS Secure Enclave and is never accessible to Peck.
  • Clipboard: Accessed only when you explicitly tap “Paste from Clipboard” or tap a Copy action.

5. User-Initiated Sharing & External Actions

  • Before sharing, you choose what text to include and whether to attach no image, a cropped excerpt, or the full original.
  • When you explicitly open a URL, phone number, map address, Calendar/Reminders entry, or system share target, only the information you selected is handed off to that destination app.

6. Your Controls & Data Deletion

  • Adjust or revoke system permissions anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Clear all local data, thumbnails, favorites, and imported copies anytime via Settings › Manage Library › Clear local data (your original Photos library items are never deleted).
  • Uninstalling Peck permanently removes all local app data from your device.

7. Updates & Contact

  • Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
  • Support & Privacy Contact: hello@sprachspiel.io

中文版（简体中文）

Peck 是一款完全在你的 iPhone 本地运行的截图整理与搜索工具，无需联网即可使用。本隐私政策与 Peck 应用内「设置 › 数据与隐私」完全一致。

1. 我们不收集任何数据

  • Peck 不提供账号系统，也不会向你索取姓名、邮箱、手机号或任何身份信息。
  • Peck 绝不向开发者或任何第三方服务器上传你的截图原图、缩略图、文字内容、搜索词、标题、摘要、收藏或编辑记录。
  • Peck 不包含任何广告 SDK、统计分析 SDK 或跨应用追踪代码（NSPrivacyTracking = false，NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes = []）。

2. 相册访问与主动添加

  • 近 30 天自动整理与更早截图按需整理：只有在你授予系统相册权限后，Peck 才会读取你允许范围内（完整授权或「仅所选照片」有限授权）的系统截图。为了省电和流畅，自动整理默认聚焦近 30 天的截图；30 天前更早的截图只显示轻量缩略卡片，等你主动点开单张时才会在你的手机本地即时整理。
  • 主动挑选或粘贴图片：通过系统照片选择器手动挑选的图片，或你主动点击「粘贴剪贴板图片」导入的图片，不受 30 天自动整理范围限制，并在 Peck 本机沙盒内保存一份独立副本。撤销系统相册权限时，Peck 会清理相册自动扫描缓存，但不会替你删掉你主动导入的独立副本（你随时可以在应用内单独移除或一键清空）。
  • iCloud 照片：如果你开启了系统的「iCloud 照片」，iOS 可能会按你的系统设置从你自己的 iCloud 下载原图到本机，这一过程由 Apple 系统直接完成，Peck 不会将图片发给任何第三方。

3. 本地处理与存储保护

  • 文字识别、内容整理与全文搜索全部在你的 iPhone 本地执行，开启飞行模式也能完整使用。
  • 本机数据库、缩略图与导入副本均启用 iOS 完整文件保护（Complete File Protection），并明确排除在设备备份之外（isExcludedFromBackup = true）。
  • 你可以在「设置 › 应用锁」开启 Face ID / Touch ID / 设备密码锁；切出应用或进入后台时，界面会自动遮盖以防任务切换器露出截图内容。

4. 系统权限说明

  • 照片（Photos）：用于自动发现并整理你允许访问的系统截图；你也可以拒绝完整访问，只通过系统选择器手动添加单张图片。
  • 提醒事项（Reminders）：只会在你主动点击「保存」创建单条提醒时按需请求权限，不会读取或修改你的其他提醒列表。
  • 日历（Calendar）：通过系统自带的日程编辑界面（EKEventEditViewController）由你核对后亲手保存，Peck 不申请读取日历权限，也不会自动或批量写入日历。
  • Face ID / 本机认证：只用于在你主动开启应用锁时解锁本机资料库，生物识别信息由 iOS 安全隔区处理，Peck 无法读取。
  • 剪贴板（Pasteboard）：只在你主动点击「粘贴剪贴板图片」或复制文字时响应你的操作，绝不在后台读取剪贴板。

5. 分享与跳转其他应用

  • 分享文字或图片前，你可以自由编辑文字，并选择「不附图片 / 选中片段 / 完整原图」。
  • 当你主动打开链接、拨打电话、查看地图、写入日历／提醒事项，或通过系统分享面板发给备忘录及其他应用时，只有你选中的内容会移交给目标应用；之后的保存与同步由目标应用及你的系统设置控制。

6. 你的控制权与数据清除

  • 你可以随时在 iOS「设置」里调整或撤销相册／提醒事项权限。
  • 你可以随时在 Peck 内前往 「设置 › 管理资料 › 清除本机资料」 一键清空所有本机记录、缩略图、收藏和主动导入的副本（绝不会删除系统相册里的原始截图）。
  • 卸载 Peck 会彻底删除应用沙盒内的全部本地数据。

7. 政策更新与联系我们

  • 若本政策因功能更新而调整，我们会在此页面及应用内同步更新生效日期。
