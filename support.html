Frequently Asked Questions (English)

Why does Peck automatically organize screenshots from the last 30 days?

To keep launch fast and protect your iPhone battery, Peck automatically organizes screenshots from the last 30 days in your Photos library. Older screenshots still appear in your timeline as lightweight cards—just tap any older screenshot to organize it on the spot.

Can I add long screenshots, regular photos, or clipboard images?

Yes. Tap the + button on the home screen to pick any image from Photos or paste an image from your clipboard. Manually added images are organized right away regardless of date and do not require full Photos access.

Does Peck upload my screenshots or need an internet connection?

No. Everything happens right on your iPhone—even in Airplane Mode. Peck has no user accounts, no ads, and collects zero personal data. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Which iPhones are supported?

Peck requires iOS 18 or later. iPhones with 8 GB RAM or more (such as iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 series, and newer) run full automatic summaries and smart organizing. iPhones with under 8 GB RAM automatically switch to Local Text Mode so text reading, full-text search, original highlighting, sharing, and quick actions all work cleanly without internet.

How do I clear Peck's local data?

Open Settings › Manage Library › Clear local data. This removes local records, thumbnails, favorites, and manually imported copies inside Peck, and never deletes original screenshots from your iPhone Photos app.

常见问题（简体中文）

为什么默认只自动整理最近 30 天的截图？

为了省电又流畅，Peck 默认自动整理相册里最近 30 天的系统截图。30 天前更早的截图依然会保留在列表里，哪张需要就点哪张，马上在你的手机本地帮你理好。

长截图、普通照片或剪贴板里的图片怎么添加？

点首页右上角的 + 按钮，可以手动从相册挑选长截图或普通照片，也可以直接粘贴剪贴板里的图片。主动添加的图片不受 30 天范围限制，也不需要开启相册完整访问权限。

Peck 会上传我的截图吗？没网能用吗？

不会上传，也不需要联网。所有识别、整理和搜索都在你的 iPhone 本地完成，开飞行模式也能照常使用。无账号、无广告、不收集任何数据，详情可查看 隐私政策。

支持哪些 iPhone 机型？

需要 iOS 18 及以上系统。8 GB 及以上内存的 iPhone（如 iPhone 15 Pro、iPhone 16 系列及更新机型）可完整使用自动摘要与智能整理；8 GB 以下内存的 iPhone 会自动切换为轻巧文字模式，本地识字、全文搜索、原图定位、复制分享和常用快捷操作一样不联网就能用。

怎么清空本机记录？会删掉我相册里的原图吗？

前往 设置 › 管理资料 › 清除本机资料 即可一键清空 Peck 本机记录、缩略图、收藏和主动导入的副本，绝对不会删除你系统相册里的原图。
